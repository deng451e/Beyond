 young woman appeared, framed in the opening, and the young man, who had been watching her with a smile, was now struck with a sudden terror.
“What is it?” he cried, in a voice which trembled with emotion.
“It is I,” said the girl, in a voice which was as sweet as the voice of a nightingale.
“You!” he stammered, “you!”
“Yes, I,” she replied, with a smile which was as sweet as the smile of a nightingale.
